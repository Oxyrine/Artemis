# Reconciliation workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: dashboard, case review, audit ledger, shared shell.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Give a financial workspace the character and hierarchy of an annual report. The user rejected the preceding achromatic layout as bland and generic; this revision intentionally replaces that world.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink navigation, cobalt actions, cool paper canvas, self-hosted Instrument Serif titles and Geist data. Open document sections and column rules replace repeated cards. A flat variance bridge uses actual explained and held paise. Statuses in records are plain text; case-header badge stacks and redundant workspace/environment labels are removed. Only the evidence viewport, floating guide and confirmation dialogs need contained surfaces.

STORY: Scan outcomes and amounts, search a receipt or customer, inspect a case's closure and evidence, then record a named decision. Verify the history separately.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 246px dark navigation column with a receipt–evidence–decision chain; 48px serif page heading; an open introduction and worked ledger example; compact agent toolbar; an asymmetric flat variance overview and outcome index separated by a rule; then the receipt queue. Primary action: cobalt Run batch. Phone: stacked sections and receipt rows separated by rules. Case review, comparison and audit share the open editorial layout.

FORM: Editorial financial reporting. User-directed replacement of the previous dealt achromatic ledger; the original seed no longer governs visual choices. Impeccable engine unavailable; no comp reference. Product-specific signatures: a live variance bridge and the evidence-to-decision workflow. All global financial totals remain stable when the queue is filtered.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
