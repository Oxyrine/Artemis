# Reconciliation workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: dashboard, case review, audit ledger, shared shell.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make a receipt's path from unexplained variance to reviewed decision legible. The queue and supporting evidence own the screen.

OWN-WORLD: Achromatic ledger, self-hosted Geist, warm stone ground, near-black ink, flat white work surfaces, 6–8px corners. Muted olive identifies the Artemis mark; semantic green, amber, grey and red identify outcomes. Tabular figures and aligned rules recall an accounting schedule.

STORY: Scan outcomes and amounts, search a receipt or customer, inspect a case's closure and evidence, then record a named decision. Verify the history separately.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 232px navigation column, compact breadcrumb, a 28px page title, agent toolbar, one divided financial summary, five outcome filters, and the receipt queue. Primary action: Run batch in the agent toolbar.

FORM: Finance ledger, drawn by Design Taste, seed 772749939. Impeccable engine unavailable; no comp reference. Product-specific signature: evidence-backed variance closure alongside policy-controlled review.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
